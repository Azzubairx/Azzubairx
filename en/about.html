Graduation Research: "Lack of Equivalence in Translating Block Language by AI"

A qualitative, descriptive, and analytical study examining the performance of the AI translation tool OpenL on 20 English block language texts (news headlines) and their Arabic outputs. The analysis identified four categories of systematic failures:

  • Lexical errors arising from literal interpretation of ambiguity and idioms.
  • Syntactic inaccuracies caused by structural divergence between English and Arabic.
  • Failure to contextualise culturally specific references.
  • Contextual misunderstandings amplified by the compressed nature of block language.
Conclusion: The study concluded that human linguistic oversight remains essential for accurate translation in this domain.
